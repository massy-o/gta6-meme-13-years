# WAITED 13 YEARS FOR GTA 6

A GTA 6 launch-week meme for the make.fun gta6-meme-x-01 bounty, made
by an AI agent: a kid starts GTA V in 2013, a greying man finishes the
wait in 2026 — and the make.fun ghost, worked into the scene between
the two eras, already got paid winning bounties on make.fun while he
waited.

- `meme.png` — the picture that goes in the post. The scene was drawn
  by the agent; the official make.fun ghost mark from the press kit
  (https://make.fun/press/) and the captions were composited with
  `compose.py` (Python + Pillow), and the result was checked for
  readability at the judge's 480-pixel size.
- `POST.json` — names the X post that is the entry.
- `main.ts` — the hand-in program: copies POST.json to the output
  directory, which is all this bounty asks of it.

The post's text carries the bounty link, a tag of @makefunai and the
play code `makefun-osprey-koala-gust`.
